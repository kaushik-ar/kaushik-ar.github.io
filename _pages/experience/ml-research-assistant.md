## Machine Learning Research Assistant
**Thiagarajar College of Engineering** | *September 2022 – May 2025*  
*Research Advisor: Dr. S. Padmavathi (spmcse@tce.edu)*  
*Focus: Explainable AI, Fairness in ML, Deep Learning*

First author on **3 published IEEE conference papers** and **2 manuscripts in progress**; 10+ citations on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=qghXVakAAAAJ&hl=en). View all publications on the <a href="{{ base_path }}/publications/" class="btn btn--info btn--small">Publications</a> page.
{: .notice--info}

- **Fair affect recognition** (submitted to *IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing*, [code](https://github.com/kaushikar11/FairAI-Emotion-Recognition)): GRU architecture for emotion classification from heterogeneous biosignals, more efficient than LSTM and Transformer baselines; evaluated DIR, Reweighing, and LFR across **11 fairness metrics** (SPD, EOD, Theil Index); LFR cut bias by **30%** at **95%** accuracy
- **Explainable affect recognition** (in preparation, [code](https://github.com/kaushikar11/Human-Emotion-Recognition-ExplainableAI)): SHAP and LIME on multimodal physiological signals (ECG, EMG, GSR, RSP, BVP); identified GSR and RSP as primary arousal indicators, with ECG and GSR driving **60%+** of arousal decisions
- **Lightweight UAV threat detection** with the Defence Institute of Advanced Technology (DIAT), Pune ([code](https://github.com/kaushikar11/DIAT-uSAT-Lightweight-Drone-classification-model)): Cross Stage Partial Network with **5×** lower complexity and **20×** faster inference; **5%** accuracy gain over SOTA across nine CNN architectures; models deployed
