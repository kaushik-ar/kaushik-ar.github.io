## MS Thesis Research — Research Assistant
**[NYU Visualization and Data Analytics (VIDA) Center](https://vida.engineering.nyu.edu/people/kaushik-alaguvadivel-ramya)** | *January 2026 – Present*  
*Advisors: Prof. Robert Krueger ([Krueger Lab](https://kruegert.github.io/lab/)) and Prof. Erdem Varol*  
*Focus: Self-Supervised Learning, 3D Biomedical Imaging, Multiplexed Tissue Imaging*

My MS thesis studies **self-supervised representation learning for 3D multiplexed tissue imaging**, working at the intersection of machine learning and biomedical imaging.

- **Self-Supervised Representation Learning** — learning useful representations from large-scale imaging data without manual labels
- **3D / Volumetric Biomedical Imaging** — working with high-dimensional, multi-channel volumetric tissue data
- **Vision Transformers & Graph Neural Networks** — modeling tissue structure and spatial context
- **Large-Scale Training on HPC** — training on large imaging datasets on a high-performance computing cluster
