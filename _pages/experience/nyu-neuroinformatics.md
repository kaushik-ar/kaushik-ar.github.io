## Summer Research, Computational Neuroscience
**Neuroinformatics Lab, New York University** | *June 2026 – July 2026*  
*Advisor: Prof. Erdem Varol*  
*Focus: Self-Supervised Learning, Neural Imaging*

- **Neuropixels spike sorting:** contributed to a self-supervised pipeline for spike localization and drift correction; audited multi-session recordings, helped resolve a cross-detector spike-matching issue, and built the training data framework for a spike-to-cluster classifier
- **Voltage imaging:** built a pipeline for sub-millisecond neural activity from low-SNR data, combining image reconstruction, motion correction, and BM4D denoising; tuned reconstruction kernels through a systematic sweep and scaled processing on HPC
